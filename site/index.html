The Promise Ledger

Twelve promises of the second Trump administration, each quoted from its primary source and measured against official data from a fixed baseline: the last data before January 20, 2025.

Foreign policy and military matters, including the war with Iran, are left out on purpose: their results are not published as regular official statistics, and any status for them would rest on editorial judgment, which this ledger is built to avoid.

What the terms mean

Each indicator
toward the promise
The value has moved in the direction the promise points, by more than the noise band.
away from the promise
The value has moved in the opposite direction, by more than the noise band.
within normal range
The change, whatever its size, is not larger than the noise band, so it cannot be told apart from the series' normal fluctuation.
stale
The newest figure available from the source is older than this indicator's age limit, counted from the end of the week or month it covers: 14 or 21 days for weekly series, 60 to 120 days for monthly series, 240 days for ICE removal records released under FOIA. This happens when a release is late, suspended or discontinued. A stale indicator stays on the page with its last figure but does not count toward its promise until newer data arrive.
no new data
Nothing has been published since the baseline.
Each promise
on track
At least half of its counted indicators are toward the promise, and none is away from it.
off track
At least half of its counted indicators are away from the promise.
mixed
Anything in between.
no current data
None of its indicators counts.
Measurement
Counted indicators
Those toward the promise, within normal range or away from it. Stale indicators and those without new data are shown but not counted.
Baseline
The last figure before January 20, 2025: December 2024 for monthly data, the last full week before the inauguration for weekly data.
Latest
The newest figure the source has published.
Noise band
The spread (standard deviation) of all changes over the same number of months or weeks in the series' own history from 2010 to 2024, leaving out March 2020 to December 2021.

Three caveats. A change after the inauguration is not proof that policy caused it: prices, energy and trade move with the world economy. The ledger measures only what the promise itself names, so it is not an overall record of the administration. A promise “on track” is neither praise nor blame.
